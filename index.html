Jarvis — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Jarvis is a personal assistant application built and used by a single person for their own accounts. It is not offered to the public, and no other users can sign in to it.

What data Jarvis accesses

With the owner's consent through Google's sign-in screen, Jarvis accesses the owner's own Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive and Google Contacts data. It uses that data only to carry out tasks the owner requests: reading and summarizing email, managing calendar events, and finding or working with files.

Where data is stored

Jarvis runs on a computer or server controlled by the owner. OAuth tokens are stored encrypted on that machine. Jarvis does not run its own cloud service and does not keep a copy of Google data anywhere else.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with third parties. The one exception: to answer the owner's requests, relevant content is sent to the AI model provider Anthropic through its API. It is used only to process those requests.

Limited Use

Jarvis's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting data

The owner can revoke Jarvis's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete the stored tokens from the machine Jarvis runs on.
